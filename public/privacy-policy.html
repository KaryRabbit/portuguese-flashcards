Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-23

European Portuguese Flashcards stores your study data locally on your device.

What data the app stores

  • flashcards you create or import
  • study session state
  • cards marked as known
  • study groups and selections
  • app preferences such as study direction

This data is stored locally on your device inside the app's local storage.

What data the app does not collect

The app does not require account creation.

The app does not collect or send personal data to our servers.

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

The app does not record your voice or collect audio data.

Pronunciation audio

The app can use your device's built-in text-to-speech features to play pronunciation audio for words and example sentences.

This uses system capabilities available on the device. The app does not upload audio or voice recordings.

Data retention

Your data remains on your device until you remove it, reset it inside the app, or delete the app.

Deleting the app may remove all locally stored study data.
