## Description
Learn European Portuguese the focused way — with flashcards built specifically for EU Portuguese (not Brazilian).

Hear every word and example sentence spoken aloud, study in small batches, and track which words you already know.

FEATURES
• Audio pronunciation for words and example sentences
• European Portuguese vocabulary with example sentences
• Verb conjugation reference (present, past, future)
• Study in focused batches and save your own study sets
• Mark cards as "known" to focus on what you're still learning
• Switch direction: English → Portuguese or Portuguese → English
• Import and export your own word lists (CSV)
• Works fully offline — no account, no sign-up, no tracking

Your data stays on your device. The app collects nothing.
